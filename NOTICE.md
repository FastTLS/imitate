# Source provenance

The initial compatibility recipes, identity algorithms and historical adapters
were migrated from the FastTLS development workspace. They preserve existing
behavior and are not new observations of real browser versions.

Historical consumer captures retain their original inventory and SHA-256
provenance in `testdata/consumer-inventory.json` and the recipe source records.
The expanded compatibility-selector fixture is a frozen migration oracle, not
the editable recipe source.
